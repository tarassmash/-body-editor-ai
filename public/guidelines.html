Content Guidelines

Last updated: {{UPDATED}}

{{APP_NAME}} helps people see themselves in a new outfit, a fitter shape or with a new tattoo. To keep it safe for everyone, these rules apply to every photo and every result.

✅ Allowed

  • Your own photos, or photos of adults who agreed to the edit.
  • Fitness, posture, proportion, outfit, skin and tattoo edits from the options in the app.

⛔ Not allowed

  • Nudity, underwear, swimwear-to-nude or any sexual or sexually suggestive content.
  • Photos of anyone under 18.
  • Editing someone without their consent, including celebrities and public figures.
  • Content that harasses, humiliates, body-shames or targets anyone.
  • Hateful, violent or illegal content.
  • Results used to deceive — fake advertising results, fake profiles, identity fraud.

How we enforce this

  • No free-text prompts. You can only choose from a fixed set of edits we designed to keep people clothed and recognisable.
  • Automatic filters. Every uploaded photo and every result is checked by an NSFW classifier. Blocked content is never shown.
  • Age & consent confirmation before every edit.
  • “AI-edited” label on every downloaded result.
  • Reporting. Use the ⚑ Report button in the editor or the support form. We review reports within 24 hours and act on them, including blocking abusive users.
