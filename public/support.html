FAQ

Do you keep my photos?
No. Photos are processed and then discarded. See the Privacy Policy.

Why was my photo blocked?
Our safety filter blocks photos with nudity or revealing content and results that might be inappropriate. Try a photo where the person is fully clothed.

The result doesn’t look like me.
Try a lower intensity, a well-lit photo and one person in the frame. If a result looks wrong or offensive, please report it.

Is there a limit?
Yes, a daily number of free edits per device/network to prevent abuse.

Is it free?
Yes, currently.
